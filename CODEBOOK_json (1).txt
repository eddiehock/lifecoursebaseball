This is the codebook for the "mlb_1968_2025_relatives.json" file.

A JSON object is organized into nested "keys." This codebook will go through each key level, down to the eventual "leaves" of data.

The outermost keys are the "player" keys. Each unique player in the dataset has a corresponding key, labeled with a text string for his name. We will use "Barry Bonds" as an example. 

The "player" keys contain three sub-keys: "bio," "career_summary," and "seasons."

"bio": this key contains biographical data (collected from Retrosheet and Fangraphs). 
    "birth_date": the date of the player's birth, recorded in character format (YYYY-MM-DD). For Bonds, "1964-07-24."
    "birth_city": the municipality of the player's birth, recorded in character format. For Bonds, "Riverside."
    "birth_state": the administrative division of the player's birth (if applicable), recorded in character format. For Bonds, "CA."
    "birth_country": the country of the player's birth, recorded in character format. For Bonds, "USA."
    "bats": does the player bat right-handed or left-handed, or is he a switch hitter? In character format, "R," "L," or "B" (for "both"). For Bonds, "L."
    "throws": does the player throw with his right or left hand? In character format, "R" or "L." For Bonds, "L."
    "height_in": the player's listed height in inches, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 73.
    "weight_lbs": the player's listed weight in pounds, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 185.
    "hall_of_fame": will be an indicator for HOF status. Currently not working correctly.

"career_summary": this key contains summary information about the player's entire career (collected from Fangraphs).
    "seasons_played": how many seasons the player played, recorded in double format. For Bonds, 22.
    "career_ops": the player's OPS (on-base percentage plus slugging percentage) over his entire career, recorded in double format. For Bonds, 1.051. 
       A common indicator of offensive success. Higher is better. Over 1.000 is elite.
    "career_g": how many games the player played in during his career, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 2986.
    "career_ab": how many at-bats the player had during his career, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 9847.
    "career_h": how many hits the player had during his career, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 2935.
    "career_hr": how many home runs the player hit during his career, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 762.
    "career_rbi": how many runs the player batted in during his career, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 1996.
    "career_bb": how many times the player was walked during his career, recorded in integer format. For Bonds, 2558.
        "Bases on balls" (BB) and "walks" are the same thing.
    "career_war": how many wins above replacement the player accumulated during his career, recorded in double format. For Bonds, 162.78.
        We use the Fangraphs-calculated WAR (fWAR). A metric of cumulative success over seasons and years, calculated by an algorithm.
    "teams": a list of the teams the player played for, by three-letter code. For Bonds, "PIT" and "SFN."

"seasons": this key contains information about every season the player played in (collected from Fangraphs and MLB). We will look at Bonds's key 16 (his sixteenth season in the league).
    "season": what year was it? In integer format, YYYY. For this season, 2001.
    "age": how old was the player when the season ended? In integer format, YY. For this season, 37.
    "multi-team": did the player play for more than one team during this season? In logical format, TRUE or FALSE. For this season, FALSE.
    "team": the team the player played for, in character format by three-letter code. For this season, "SFN."
    "lg": whether the team was in the American or National League. In character format, "AL" or "NL." For this season, "NL."
    "stats": a list of the player's numerical stats for this season. The counting stats are all in integer format, and the average stats are all in double format.

        COUNTING STATS
        "G": how many games did the player play in this season? For 2001 Bonds, 153.
        "AB": how many at-bats did the player have this season? For 2001 Bonds, 476.  
        "PA": how many plate appearances did the player have this season? For 2001 Bonds, 664.
            A plate appearance that ends in a walk, hit-by-pitch, or sacrifice play is not recorded as an at-bat.
        "H": how many hits did the player have this season? For 2001 Bonds, 156.
        "2B": how many doubles did the player have this season? For 2001 Bonds, 32.
        "3B": how many triples did the player have this season? For 2001 Bonds, 2.
        "HR": how many doubles did the player have this season? For 2001 Bonds, 73.
        "R": how many runs did the player score this season? For 2001 Bonds, 129.
        "RBI": how many runs did the player bat in this season? For 2001 Bonds, 137.
        "BB": how many times was the player walked this season? For 2001 Bonds, 177.
        "SO": how many times did the player strike out this season? For 2001 Bonds, 93.

        AVERAGE STATS
        "BA": what was the player's batting average this season? For 2001 Bonds, 0.328.
            The percentage of at-bats where the player got a hit is the batting average (or simply H/AB).
        "OBP": what was the player's on-base percentage this season? For 2001 Bonds, 515.
            The percentage of plate appearances where the player got on base is the on-base percentage.
        "SLG": what was the player's slugging percentage this season? For 2001 Bonds, 0.863.
            Slugging percentage is total bases (on hits) per at-bat (or (H+2*2B+3*3B+4*HR)/AB).
        "OPS": what was the player's OPS this season? For 2001 Bonds, 1.378.
            A common indicator of offensive success. Higher is better. Over 1.000 is elite. Simply calculated as OBP+SLG.
        "WAR": how many wins above replacement did the player account for this season? For 2001 Bonds, 11.86.
            A common indicator of overall player-season quality. A metric of cumulative success, calculated with the Fangraphs algorithm.

"id1": an ID for player-matching, recorded in character format. For Bonds, "bondb101." (For his father Bobby Bonds, "bondb001.")
